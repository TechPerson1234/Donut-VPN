Trust & transparency

Built to be trusted, not just believed.

A VPN asks you to hand over your traffic. That's a big ask. Here's how we earn it — and how you can check.

Strict zero-logs

We don't record your browsing history, DNS queries, connection timestamps or the IP addresses you connect from. There is nothing to hand over because nothing is stored.

Independently audited

Our clients and infrastructure are reviewed by third-party security firms. We publish the full results — including anything we got wrong — so you don't have to take our word for it.

Encrypted by default

Every mode ships with strong modern ciphers and perfect forward secrecy. There is no "insecure mode" hidden in a settings menu somewhere.

No ads, no trackers

We don't sell data, we don't run ad networks and we don't embed third-party trackers in our apps or on this website. Donut VPN is free for everyone, and it stays that way.

Warrant canary

A signed transparency statement is refreshed regularly. If it ever stops updating, you know something happened — without us having to say a word.

Honest about limits

A VPN is a privacy tool, not magic. We tell you exactly what each mode protects against — and what it can't — instead of promising total anonymity.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Who we are

Donut VPN ("we", "us") provides free encrypted network privacy tools. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit this website or use our applications and services.

2. What we do not collect

  • Your browsing history or the sites you visit
  • Your DNS queries
  • Connection timestamps or session durations
  • Your original IP address in connection with your activity
  • Traffic contents of any kind

We design our systems so this data is never written to disk in the first place. You cannot lose what was never recorded.

3. What we do collect

  • Account data: the email address you provide, and a hashed authentication credential. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Support messages: anything you send us directly, kept so we can resolve your issue.
  • Aggregate diagnostics: opt-in crash reports and app version data, stripped of identifying information before transmission.
  • Website analytics: privacy-respecting, aggregate page counts with no cookies, no fingerprinting and no cross-site tracking.

4. How we use it

To create and secure your account, provide support, keep the service running and prevent abuse. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only with processors strictly necessary to run the service (email delivery, infrastructure hosting), each bound by contract, and with authorities only where we are legally compelled and where a valid order exists. Because we hold no activity logs, there is no activity data to compel.

6. Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of closure. Support tickets are kept for 24 months. Aggregate diagnostics are kept in non-identifying form only.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise any of these. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access controls, and regular independent review. No system is perfect; if a breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you without undue delay.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced by email or in-app.

Questions

Things people ask us

When does Donut VPN launch?

We're in active development and haven't locked a public date yet. Check back here — this page will be the first place we announce it.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every mode, every feature, every platform. There is no premium tier, no trial period, no credit card screen and no feature held back behind a paywall. Donut VPN is free for everyone and stays that way.

Can I use all four VPN modes?

Yes. All four modes — Bypass Firewall, Normal Secure, iOS/Android Local Tunnel and TOR — are available to everyone. You switch between them from the same app, and you can switch instantly without reinstalling anything.

Do you really keep zero logs?

Yes. Our infrastructure is built so that activity data is never written to persistent storage. We can't hand over logs we don't have. Our privacy policy spells out exactly what account data we do hold, and why.

How can I trust closed-source software?

Our clients are not open source, so you can't read the code yourself. Instead, we commission independent security firms to audit our apps and infrastructure and publish their findings in full. We also maintain a public warrant canary and document exactly what data we hold. That's a different trust model from open source, and we'd rather be upfront about it than pretend otherwise.

How many devices can I connect at once?

Final device limits are still being decided. Our aim is to cover everything a normal person owns — phone, laptop, tablet — without making you count or pay extra.

Which platforms will be supported?

iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux are all planned for launch, with native clients tailored to each platform.

Is a VPN enough to stay anonymous?

No single tool is. A VPN hides your traffic from your network and masks your IP from sites you visit, but logging into an account still identifies you. Our TOR mode adds multi-hop onion routing for much stronger unlinkability, and we'll always be honest about the limits of each mode.
